What people ask the City to publish

Anyone can nominate a dataset through the Open Data help desk, and the City publishes the whole queue: the request in the asker's own words, the agency it was routed to, and the answer that came back. It is the portal's own suggestion box, read from the outside — a running record of the gap between what the City puts out and what people actually want from it. The City gives itself 60 days to answer.
